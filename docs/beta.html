Zenith is in active beta development. Features, appearance and compatibility can change between builds.

Where can I download Zenith?

The public DMG has not been posted to this showcase yet. When the beta package is ready, it will be linked here and in GitHub Releases. No download, price or release date is being promised today.

Which Macs is this beta designed for?

The current build targets macOS 26 or later on Apple silicon. A physical camera notch is not required for floating presentations. Lid effects, battery information, fan controls and some other features depend on the Mac and attached devices. Intel compatibility is not currently advertised.

Do notifications, replies and calls work with every app?

No. These are experimental integrations. They need the corresponding macOS permissions and usable controls in the source app. Native macOS banners may still appear, and reply or call actions may be unavailable. The beta does not promise universal WhatsApp, Telegram, FaceTime, Zoom, Teams or browser-call support.

What permissions may I need?

Enable access for the features you choose. Notification and call integrations may require Accessibility or Device Control and Data Access. Camera tools need Camera access. Calendar, audio, automation and display effects may require their corresponding system permissions. Review prompts in macOS before granting access.

Is the application open source?

No. Zenith is proprietary software created by Harsh Paun. The public GitHub repository contains product information and branding. Application source, internal assets and implementation details are not published there.

Is Zenith made by Apple?

No. Zenith is an independent app by Harsh Paun. References to the Mac notch, liquid glass or Dynamic Island describe the experience; they do not imply an Apple partnership or endorsement.

How do I report a beta issue?

Open a GitHub issue with your Zenith version, macOS version, Mac model and a short description. Remove personal messages, account details and other private information from screenshots. Please do not attach credentials or unfiltered logs.

What data does this website collect?

This showcase adds no analytics scripts, advertising, sign-up form or tracking cookies. The hosting provider may process ordinary request information to deliver the site. GitHub and App Store links open third-party services with their own policies. This statement covers this website, not Zenith or the other apps’ privacy practices.
