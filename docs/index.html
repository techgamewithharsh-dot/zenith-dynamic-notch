SOUND THAT REMEMBERS

Your sound.
Automatically.

Save one EQ for your headphones and another for your Mac speakers. Zenith switches Apple Music’s sound for you when your audio output changes.

  1. Set the sound in Apple Music.
  2. Save it for that device in Zenith.
  3. Switch devices. Your saved sound follows.
What you need

Turn on automatic switching in Zenith. Music and Accessibility access are required. Devices without a saved profile leave the EQ unchanged. Music’s per-song EQ and Music-only AirPlay routes can affect the result.

What changed in this release?

Saved Apple Music EQ profiles for each output, live keyboard previews, a new onboarding Highlights chapter, a rebuilt Minecraft arrival with wider character spacing, and a separate signed updater that finishes installation after Zenith closes.

How do automatic updates work?

Keep Zenith running and online in a writable Applications folder. The updater verifies the release, replaces the app, and keeps the old copy until the new one launches. Older builds with disabled checks or installation restrictions may need the separate updater or the DMG. Your settings stay in place.

Which Mac and permissions do I need?

This build supports Apple silicon Macs on macOS 26 or later. A physical notch is not required. Onboarding guides the required input permissions; camera, audio and other integrations need their own macOS approvals. Read compatibility and beta notes ↗
